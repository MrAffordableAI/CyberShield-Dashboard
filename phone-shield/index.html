I think I've been hacked

Follow these steps from a trusted device when possible:

  1. Stop interacting with the suspicious message or caller.
  2. For financial fraud, contact your bank using its official app or a number on your card.
  3. Secure your primary email and Apple/Google account; sign out unknown sessions and enable multifactor authentication.
  4. Install official system updates and review unfamiliar apps and account recovery methods.
  5. If money or identity information was stolen, report it to appropriate authorities and consider a credit freeze.

Do not rely on this checklist as proof your device is malware-free.
